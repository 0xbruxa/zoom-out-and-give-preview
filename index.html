Why sit down

Year-end giving can turn into one ask at a time.

An email here, a letter there, a few quick yeses before December 31. This year, start with your people. Decide together what you want your giving to do, then give with a plan.

Questions

Before you sit down.

Do we need a donor-advised fund?

No. The worksheet works on its own. If you decide to open one, you can do it in the Endaoment App, with no minimum to start.

Who sees our answers?

Only you. Your answers save in this browser and are never sent to us. When you email your plan, it goes out from your own email app. We keep only your email address.

How long does it take?

About 30 minutes for the page. Longer if the conversation gets good.

What's the catch with the gift?

There isn't one. Fill out the worksheet, email yourself the plan, and tell us one thing that came out of it. We'll mail an Endaoment hat for everyone at the table, up to four, with a personal thank-you card. US addresses only, while supplies last.

I'm an advisor. Can I use this with clients?

Yes. Send clients the link before your year-end meeting, or fill it out together. In the last step, add their email and the plan goes to both of you.
