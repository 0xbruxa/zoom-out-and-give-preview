Why sit down

Your giving deserves a plan.

Most investors say they want guidance on their giving. Most still give without a structured plan, and the talk about what matters most to your family keeps waiting for next year. Zoom Out & Give gives it a simple, easy and fun starting point.

Based on T. Rowe Price research, The Generosity Effect, February 2026.

How it works

Three simple (and fun) steps

  1. 1

Questions

Before you sit down.

Do we need a donor-advised fund?

No. The worksheet works on its own. If you decide to open one, you can do it in the Endaoment App, with no minimum to start.

Who sees our answers?

Only you, and anyone you email your plan to. Your answers save on this device. When you email your plan, we send it for you and don't keep a copy.

What's the catch with the gift?

There isn't one. Fill out the worksheet, email yourself the plan, and tell us about your experience. We'll mail a very sweet Endaoment hat + goodies for everyone at the table, with personal touches from our team. For now, we can only ship to US addresses, while supplies last!

I'm an advisor. Can I use this with clients?

Yes!! This was made exactly for advisors and families, to give you a simple process to start the giving conversation early. In the last step, add their email and the plan goes to both of you.

For advisors: send this to clients before year-end to start the conversation.
