Why sit down

Your giving deserves a strategy.

Three in four investors want guidance on their giving from their advisor, yet only a third receive it. Meanwhile, the talk about what matters most to your family keeps waiting for next year. Zoom Out & Give is an enjoyable pause to plan.

Based on T. Rowe Price research, The Generosity Effect, February 2026.

Questions

Before you sit down.

Do we need a donor-advised fund?

No. The worksheet works on its own. If you decide to open one, you can do it in the Endaoment App, with no minimum to start.

Who sees our answers?

Only you, and anyone you email your plan to. Your answers save on this device. When you email your plan, we send it for you and don't keep a copy.

What's the catch with the gift?

There isn't one. Fill out the worksheet, email yourself the plan, and tell us how it went. US addresses only, while supplies last.

I'm an advisor. Can I use this with clients?

Yes. Send clients the link before your year-end meeting, or fill it out together. In the last step, add their email and the plan goes to both of you.
